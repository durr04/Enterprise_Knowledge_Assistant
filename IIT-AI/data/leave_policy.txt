Leave Policy - Acme Corp

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
credited at the start of the year on a pro-rata basis for new joiners.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next
calendar year. Carried-forward leave must be used within the first quarter (Jan-Mar) of
the new year, after which it will lapse.

4. Leave Application Process
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in
advance, except in emergencies. Manager approval is required before leave is confirmed.

5. Public Holidays
Acme Corp observes 12 public holidays per year as per the government holiday calendar
published annually by HR.

6. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid leave. Paternity leave is 2 weeks paid leave, to be
availed within 3 months of the child's birth.
