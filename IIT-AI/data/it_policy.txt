IT Policy - Acme Corp

1. Acceptable Use
Company IT assets (laptops, email, VPN) must be used primarily for work purposes.
Limited personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with productivity
or violate any other company policy.

2. VPN Access
All employees accessing company systems remotely must use the company-approved VPN
client. VPN credentials are issued by the IT team during onboarding.

3. Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long, include a mix of uppercase, lowercase,
numbers and symbols, and must be changed every 90 days. Passwords must not be shared.

4. VPN Password Reset
To reset a VPN password, employees should raise a request via the IT Service Portal or
contact the IT Helpdesk at ithelpdesk@acmecorp.com. A temporary password is issued
within 4 business hours, and must be changed on first login.

5. Data Security
Confidential company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud
storage accounts. All company laptops must have disk encryption enabled.

6. Software Installation
Employees must not install unlicensed or unapproved software on company devices.
Requests for new software must be raised with IT for review and approval.
